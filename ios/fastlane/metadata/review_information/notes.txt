No account or login is required. All features are available from the first launch.
The app shows optional rewarded ads (Google AdMob) only when the user taps a "watch ad" button. Ads are configured as child-directed and non-personalized for all users.
In-app purchases (gems, Starter Pack, Fleet Commander Pass) are optional; the whole game can be played without paying. Restore Purchases is in the Shop.
The UI supports 10 languages and follows the device language (it can also be changed in Settings).
