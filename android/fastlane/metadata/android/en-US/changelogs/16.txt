New: Land & Explore! Land on a planet and drive a rover for 2-3 minutes. Dig up Ruins, search a Crash site, destroy the Alien nest and mine a Rare vein. Get back to your lander before your oxygen runs out to keep all your loot. Vein found, Nest cleared and Fully surveyed stay as permanent bonuses for that planet's colony. Garden, desert, ice and lava planets each have their own terrain and hazards. The Codex has a new Relics tab. Fixed goal card overflow on small screens.
